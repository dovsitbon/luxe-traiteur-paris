IIWeddings

On the day, every moment has its table

A wedding reception unfolds in stages. We plan the timeline, the menu and the setting of each one with you, so your guests move from one to the next without ever waiting.

  1. I

    The drinks reception

    Canapés, food stations and dressed buffets, a fresh juice bar and soft drinks, served while the photos are taken.

  2. II

    The dinner

    Plated service or grand buffets, depending on your venue and guest count. Starter, main, cheese: the menu is built with you.

  3. III

    Dessert

    Wedding cake, a dessert buffet, petits fours and coffee, to close the evening on a beautiful sweet table.

VIOur craft

Every service precisely timed, every flavour mastered

A beautiful reception leaves nothing to chance. Before the day, we set the timeline with you minute by minute, the number of canapés per guest, where the buffets go and what each member of the team will do.

Everything is prepared and plated by our own kitchen. In the room, our head waiters know the menu, any allergies you've flagged and the key moments of the evening. All you have to think about is your guests.

  • Planning

    A written timeline, approved by you and shared with the whole team.

  • Service

    Uniformed head waiters, attentive and discreet.

  • Flavour

    Tailored menus, generous and carefully made.

The art of cooking… or of defending yourself against melted butter.
